ESP-SDR firmware installer

Flash via USB Serial/JTAG or a USB-to-UART adapter. For the correlator, UART adapters must support 2 MBaud.

Before connecting

  1. Open this page in a browser with WebSerial or WebUSB support.
  2. If connection fails, hold down the BOOT button on your board while connecting.
  3. On Linux, check udev rules / serial permissions if access is denied.
